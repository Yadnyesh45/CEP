About the project

Project: Community Engagement Project, Second Year, Department of Computer Engineering, Sinhgad College of Engineering, Vadgaon, Pune.

Problem: Many school students lack proper guidance about careers, courses, entrance exams, scholarships and learning resources, so they find it hard to make informed decisions.

Aim: Put reliable career and education information in one simple, mobile-friendly website.

  • Help students explore careers and compare pathways after 10th and 12th.
  • Point students to official scholarship portals and free learning resources.
  • Support awareness sessions in nearby schools and collect feedback.

Beneficiaries: Students of Classes 8–12, parents and teachers.
